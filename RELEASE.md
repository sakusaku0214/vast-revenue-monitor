# Vast Revenue Monitor v1.1.2

This hotfix makes config-relative state and log paths independent of the caller's working directory and extends explicit repair to reconstruct Hourly ATH from corrected positive interval increments. It also preserves the accounting consistency behavior that calculates Today from the balance at the JST 09:00 boundary, keeps Yesterday fixed, and defines payout weeks as Saturday 09:00-to-Saturday 09:00. A payout month contains the four or five completed payout weeks assigned to it plus its current running week. Hourly ATH updates immediately; Daily, Weekly, and Monthly ATHs use completed periods only. The explicit, backup-first repair command can detect and rebuild invalid Hourly and completed-period ATHs, but no repair is ever applied automatically.
